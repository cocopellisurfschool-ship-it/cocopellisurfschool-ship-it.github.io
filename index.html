Good To Know

Questions, Answered

Do I need any experience?

No. We teach complete beginners and non-swimmers, starting with technique and safety on the sand.

Best time to visit?

October to May is ideal for beginners. Before October and after May (monsoon) can be challenging.

How big are the groups?

Very small groups or private one-on-one lessons.

Can I rent or buy a board?

Yes — long boards, short boards, bodyboards, wetsuits, plus buying, selling, exchange and ding repairs.

Do you offer surf trips?

Yes, along India's hidden coastline. Let us know in advance.
